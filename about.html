About the Firm

Founder-led. Data-driven. Built for public programs.

Who We Are

A New York small business bringing analyst-grade structure to program operations

McKenzie Consulting Firm, LLC is a woman-owned small business based in New York. We help government programs, and the prime contractors that serve them, keep their records complete, their workflows consistent, and their reporting accurate. Every engagement starts with the same question: what does leadership need to see, and what has to be true in the files for that report to hold up?

JM

Jessica McKenzie

Founder & Principal

Jessica founded MCF to bring the discipline of data analysis to the administrative work public programs depend on. She works in SQL, Python, BigQuery, Excel, Tableau, and Power BI, and has hands-on experience pulling data out of operational systems and loading it into relational databases for reporting.

Her background spans federal administrative work as an Administrative Clerk with U.S. Customs and Border Protection, legal operations analysis, and marketing analytics consulting. That mix gives her a working view of how documentation, deadlines, and data meet in regulated settings.
