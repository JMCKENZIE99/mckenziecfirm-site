Teaming & Subcontracting

A ready small-business partner for reporting, records, and program operations workstreams.

For Prime Contractors

Defined workstreams, delivered under your management and on your schedule

Prime contractors bring MCF in to own specific workstreams on federal, state, and city contracts. We work inside your management structure, follow your quality processes, and report on the cadence you set. You get a registered small business partner with the tools and templates already built.

Workstreams We Take On

Data Migration & Reporting

Data extraction, cleanup, validation, and loading into reporting databases (SQL, Python, BigQuery).

Dashboards & KPI Reporting

Program performance dashboards and weekly or monthly reporting packages in Power BI, Tableau, or Excel.

Document Digitization & Records

Records preparation, scanning coordination, searchable PDF/OCR workflows, indexing, and quality checks.

Program & Administrative Support

Intake, case tracking, correspondence, billing administration, and SOP development.

Personnel Staffing & Placement

Recruiting, screening, and placing administrative and clerical staff, including on-site support at federal facilities.

Why Team With MCF

  • Small business credentials. Woman-owned small business, active in SAM.gov, with UEI and CAGE in place.
  • Set-aside support. On qualifying small business set-asides, MCF may count as a similarly situated entity toward your limitations on subcontracting.
  • Built-in delivery tools. Trackers, SOPs, and dashboard templates ready to adapt to your contract's requirements. See our sample systems.
  • Fast response. Teaming inquiries answered within one business day.

How Teaming Works

  1. Share the opportunity. Send the solicitation number and the scope you want covered.
  2. Fit check. We confirm fit, capacity, and NAICS alignment within one business day.
  3. Teaming agreement. We sign your NDA and teaming agreement and send our capability package.
  4. Proposal support. We provide the workstream approach, staffing plan, and pricing inputs for our portion.
  5. Delivery. After award, we deliver and report to your program manager on the agreed schedule.
